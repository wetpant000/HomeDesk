Guide

How to use HomeDesk.

Five minutes to set up. After that, HomeDesk remembers the dates and chases the replies for you.

Install it on your phone

  1. Android (Chrome): open HomeDesk, tap ⋮, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  2. iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. It opens full screen like any app, with the camera ready for bills.

File your documents

  1. Open Add and take a photo of a bill, invoice, warranty card or ID proof. A PDF works too.
  2. Or paste the text of an order-confirmation or bill email.
  3. Check the details HomeDesk read, fix anything wrong and tap Save.

Check Today

Today lists what needs attention in the next 30 days: warranties ending, renewals, bill due dates, unusual bills and complaints with no reply. Tap Add dates to calendar to get reminders on your phone's calendar.

Report a problem

  1. Something broke? Tap Report a problem on its invoice and describe it in one line.
  2. HomeDesk checks the warranty and drafts the email with the purchase details.
  3. In Cases, edit the draft if you like and tap Approve & send. Your mail app opens with it ready.

Follow up and escalate

HomeDesk reminds you after 7 days. No reply? Tap Escalate for a firmer second email that mentions the National Consumer Helpline (1915). Tap Mark resolved when it's done.

Find anything

Search the Vault with everyday words, like fridge, passport or power bill. Open a document to see its file, warranty status and full history.

Ask Claude

Connect HomeDesk to Claude Desktop (see the README) and just ask:

What's due this month? My AC stopped cooling, start a complaint.

Claude can draft, but only you can approve and send.

Use your voice Soon

Look for the mic buttons. Soon you'll be able to:

  • Tap Read aloud on Today to hear what's due.
  • Speak a problem instead of typing it.
  • Search the Vault by voice.
  • Tap the round mic and say "What's due this week?"

Questions

Does anything get sent without me?

No. HomeDesk only drafts. Every email waits in Cases until you tap Approve & send, and then it opens in your own mail app.

What if “AI reading off” shows at the top?

HomeDesk still works: it stores your files and reads pasted text with simple rules. You fill in or correct the details. With an Anthropic API key set, Claude reads photos and PDFs for you.

Where is my data kept?

In a database and folder on the computer running HomeDesk. Only the document you are adding is sent to Claude to be read, and only when AI reading is on.

How do I switch between dark and light?

Tap the Light / Dark button at the top. HomeDesk follows your phone's setting until you choose, then remembers your choice.
